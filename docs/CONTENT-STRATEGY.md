Last updated: 2026-08-08

## 1. Current Content Inventory

walidhasan.com content is 100% commercial/service-page copy: homepage, services, case studies list, about, tools, contact, booking, one confirmed portfolio detail page, an SEO report generator tool, and legal pages. There is **no blog, no articles, no guides, no opinion/POV content, and no long-form evergreen content** anywhere on the domain or off it.

## 2. Why This Matters for Entity + AI Visibility

Commercial pages answer "what do you sell" — they rarely get cited by AI answer engines or earn organic backlinks, because they read as sales copy rather than as a source of information. Google's entity understanding and AI models' citation behavior both favor pages that state clear, quotable, fact-shaped claims ("X years of experience," "built Y," "specializes in Z niche") independent of a sales pitch. Case studies and a knowledge-style About page are the closest existing assets to this; a blog/insights section is the missing engine for producing more of them at scale.

## 3. Recommended Content Pillars (draft — confirm before implementation)

1. **Case study deep-dives** — turn each of the 42 portfolio entries into its own page: problem, approach, specific numbers, screenshots, client quote, link to the live site where possible. This alone is 40+ new indexable, name-anchored, link-worthy pages using content he already has the raw material for.
2. **Local SEO playbooks** — practical guides (e.g., "How we got [niche] businesses into the Google Map Pack") that double as Inoviqa lead-gen content and personal-authority content.
3. **Tool/build logs** — short posts on building ReplyChief, Site Audit Pilot, RFQ AutoPilot, Shopify AdminPalette: what problem each solves, why it was built, lessons learned. Product + founder story content performs well for both SEO and AI-citation ("what has Walid Hasan built").
4. **Opinion / POV pieces** on web design, SEO, and analytics trends — the type of content that gets cited, shared, and linked by other sites, and is the standard format used by peer personal brands (see COMPETITOR-RESEARCH.md §3).
5. **"About/FAQ" expansion** — richer, fact-dense About page content answering the direct questions AI engines are likely to be asked about him (see KEYWORD-STRATEGY.md §5), written as clear declarative statements rather than only marketing language.

## 4. Content Format Recommendations

- Every piece should carry a consistent byline ("Written by Walid Hasan, founder of Inoviqa") with a linked author bio box including headshot and sameAs-consistent social links.
- Every case study and article should link internally to /about and to the relevant service page, and externally to the live client site where permission/possible (mutually reinforcing SEO and relationship value).
- Video is currently underused: the YouTube channel exists with real tutorial content but is not referenced anywhere in the walidhasan.com content inventory captured in this audit — embedding relevant videos in case studies/articles would tie the two assets together.
